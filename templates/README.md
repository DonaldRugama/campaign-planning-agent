# Upload example
1. Upload campaign_brief.json using the brief control, then click Load brief into form.
2. Upload product_notes.txt, brand_notes.txt and measurement_notes.txt using the document control.
3. Paste this exact product fact into Approved product facts: It costs $30 and comes in white and black.
4. Confirm you reviewed the fact, select practice mode and generate.
5. Then test AI mode. Both modes use your uploaded sources. Changing form values alone does not replace demo facts.

Briefs contain all six required fields. You can also enter them manually. Documents must be UTF-8 .txt or .md (up to 10, 200 KB each, 500 KB total). The brief limit is 20 KB. PDF/Word ingestion and freeform brief parsing are not included. Keep quotes short enough to fit a retrieved passage.
